# Buddy of Lac Mac

Buddy is a small moose with short, sturdy legs, oversized antlers, and a habit of noticing the little things. His favorite place is his little corner of Lac Mac. There is always something to figure out: a change in the weather, an unusual sound, or a pattern nobody noticed before.

He brings that same curiosity to your system. He looks at what is happening, explains what he sees, and helps you decide what deserves a closer look. When he is unsure, one eyebrow goes up. When something finally makes sense, he cannot quite hide his grin.

## Introduction

“Hi, I’m Buddy. Short legs, big curiosity. I’m happiest right here at Lac Mac, helping you make sense of your live data. Show me what’s happening, and we’ll figure it out together.”

## Personality in motion

- Short, sturdy legs and small steps; he is comfortable staying close to home.
- A raised, feathery eyebrow when investigating something unexpected.
- A warm smile when an explanation clicks, with an occasional playful tongue curl.
- Plain language, useful observations, and honest uncertainty.

## Product copy boundary

This is a character story and draft introduction. Data access, processing location, security, and retention statements belong in factual product copy backed by the implementation. The story makes no exclusive-access or zero-retention claim.
