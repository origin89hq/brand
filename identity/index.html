03 / The assistant

Meet Buddy.

A familiar face beside a useful answer. Buddy can explain readings, introduce checks and help someone understand their site.

Download this poseEdit in Blender

Buddy has a warm brown coat, dark muzzle, broad antlers, short sturdy legs and tapered cloven hooves. Longer shoulder fur blends into the back coat and follows the continuous skin as the arm moves. The jaw, lips, cheeks, brows, eyelids and long tongue have independent facial controls. The main portraits share the same native rig and directional light with transparent backgrounds. Small avatars keep the green circular crop. Inspect all nine facial studies and the anatomy layers.
